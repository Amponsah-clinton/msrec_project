About MSREC

An independent committee, built on documented principle.

MSREC's work rests on a clear overview, a documented history and a defined vision, mission and mandate — explore each below.

MSREC (MetaScholar Research Ethics Committee) is a multidisciplinary committee established under MetaScholar Limited to review ethically sensitive research and support responsible research conduct. It safeguards the rights, dignity, safety and wellbeing of research participants while supporting rigorous, ethically sound research across health, social science, engineering, artificial intelligence and evidence-synthesis disciplines.

Every application moves through a documented, transparent pathway — from submission and screening, through independent review and decision, to post-approval monitoring — so outcomes remain consistent, accountable and verifiable.

MSREC was established to formalize ethics oversight for MetaScholar-affiliated researchers and partner institutions. What began as a single review panel has grown into a structured committee with defined governance, documented standard operating procedures and a transparent, repeatable review pathway.

Until formal external registrations are obtained, MSREC's historical content avoids language implying national accreditation or regulator endorsement. That evolution continues today as MSREC extends its multidisciplinary review capacity to new research domains, including AI, ICT and evidence-synthesis work.

To be a trusted, independent and technology-enabled research ethics committee that promotes participant protection, responsible innovation and high standards of research integrity.

To provide timely, competent, transparent and impartial ethical review; support responsible research practice; monitor approved studies; and maintain secure, verifiable and accountable ethics-review records across diverse disciplines.

MSREC's mandate authorizes it to determine whether a project requires ethics review; undertake expedited or full Committee review where appropriate; require modifications; approve or decline protocols; and review amendments.

The mandate further covers continuing oversight, receiving safety and deviation reports, suspending or withdrawing approval when necessary, and issuing study-closure determinations — all defined in the MSREC Charter and SOPs.

Ethical Principles

Every review is grounded in four principles, consistent with WHO, CIOMS and ICH E6(R3) guidance on research ethics governance.

Respect for Persons

Voluntary participation, meaningful information, valid consent and respect for withdrawal rights.

Beneficence

Proportionate risk minimization and careful assessment of foreseeable harms and benefits.

Justice

Fair participant selection and avoidance of unjustified exclusion, exploitation or burden concentration.

Transparency & Accountability

Documented procedures, traceable decisions, conflict management and auditable records.

Governance

Board, Committee & Secretariat

MSREC operates with clear separation between institutional governance and protocol-specific ethical decision-making, so that ethics decisions remain protected from commercial or managerial influence.

Board

Institutional governance

MetaScholar Limited's Board provides institutional governance, legal support, financial stewardship and resources. It does not direct or reverse protocol-specific ethics decisions.

Organizational Structure
MSREC Research Ethics Committee
Board Governing Body
Committee Review Body
Secretariat Administrative Body

The Board, Committee and Secretariat operate as distinct but coordinated bodies, each with defined responsibilities, to preserve checks and balances throughout the review process.

Protocol-specific decisions are protected from commercial, managerial, sponsor or applicant pressure. Committee members with an actual, potential or perceived conflict of interest must disclose it and be recused where required.
