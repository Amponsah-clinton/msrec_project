Conflict & Recusal

Before you read or discuss a protocol, tell the committee whether you have a conflict of interest in it. Every protocol needs a declaration from you, even if the answer is “none”.

When should you declare a conflict?

Declare if anything could reasonably make your judgement of this study seem less than impartial, for example:

  • You or a close relative has a financial interest in the study, its sponsor or its outcome;
  • You supervise, or are supervised by, or closely collaborate with the investigators;
  • You work in the same department or institution as the study team.

If you recuse yourself you step back from the protocol entirely. If you ask the Chair to decide, you stay out of the discussion until they rule.
