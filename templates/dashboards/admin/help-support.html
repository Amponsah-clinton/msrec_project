Frequently Asked Questions

Common admin tasks and where to find them.

How do I approve a Reviewer or Committee Member request?

Go to Users & Roles, open the "Pending" tab, and use Approve or Reject on each request. Their CV (if attached at signup) is linked from the same row so you can review it before deciding. Approving sends the applicant an automatic email and unlocks their new dashboard immediately.

How do I change a review fee?

Open Finance and edit the amount next to any review pathway in the Fee Schedule. The change applies immediately to new checkouts and to the fee note shown on the application form -- nothing already paid is affected.

How do I suspend or delete an account?

From Users & Roles, open the account's row menu. Suspending signs them out of every active session immediately and blocks further sign-in without deleting anything. Deleting is permanent -- it also removes their uploaded files from Storage -- and can't be undone.

Where can I see platform-wide numbers for a board report?

Reports & Analytics has applications by status and review pathway, monthly submission volume, user counts by role, and payment totals -- use "Export CSV" on that page to download the same numbers.

How do I keep my own account secure?

Turn on an authenticator app or SMS backup codes under Profile & Security, and check Access & Security occasionally to see every staff session that's currently signed in -- revoke anything you don't recognise from Profile & Security's Active Sessions list.
