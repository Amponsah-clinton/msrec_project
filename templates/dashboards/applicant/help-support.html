Frequently Asked Questions

The questions applicants ask us most often.

How long does ethics review typically take?

Full Committee review usually takes 4–6 weeks from a complete submission. Expedited reviews for minimal-risk studies typically take 1–2 weeks. You can track the exact status of your application under Applications → Under Review.

What should I do if I need to amend an approved study?

Submit an Amendment Request under Post-Approval → Amendments describing the proposed change and rationale. Minor administrative amendments are typically reviewed within a week; substantive changes may require Full Committee review.

How do I report an adverse event?

Serious or unexpected adverse events must be reported within 5 working days via Post-Approval → Adverse Events. Include the event type, date of onset, relatedness to the study, and outcome.

Can I add a co-investigator after approval?

Yes. Use the Invite Team Member form under Research Team to add a co-investigator or research assistant. Adding personnel with access to identifiable data may require an amendment to be filed as well.

How do I pay my review fee?

Go to Payments → Make Payment and choose Mobile Money, Bank Transfer, or Card. Once confirmed, a receipt is generated automatically and can be downloaded from Payments → Receipts.
