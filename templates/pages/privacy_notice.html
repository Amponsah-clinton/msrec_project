Legal · Privacy

What we collect to run an ethics review, and nothing beyond that.

An ethics application involves real personal and professional detail — yours, and sometimes your research participants'. This notice explains exactly what MSREC collects at each step, who inside (and outside) the Committee can see it, how long we keep it, and how to ask us for a copy, a correction, or a deletion.

01

Who we are

MSREC, run under Metascholar Limited (Ghana), is the controller for the data described here.

The Metascholar Research Ethics Committee ("MSREC", "we", "us") is established under Metascholar Limited, based in Ghana. This notice applies to the MSREC platform — the public website, applicant and reviewer dashboards, and Secretariat/Committee/admin tools — and covers everyone who uses it: applicants, reviewers, committee members, and visitors to the public pages.

It should be read alongside our Terms of Use and the Data Protection and Records policies published on our Governance page, which govern retention and access in more procedural detail.

02

Information we collect

Only what a step actually requires — a fuller profile if you're asking to review, application content if you're applying.

At signup, from everyone

  • Name, title, email, phone, country of residence, and highest qualification;
  • Institution, department, position, and institutional address (where applicable);
  • A password, stored only as a salted, one-way hash — we never see or store it in plain text.

If you request Reviewer or Committee access

  • Professional background: position, institution, years of experience, prior review/committee experience, areas of expertise, ORCID and professional registration numbers;
  • A short professional bio and CV;
  • Declarations of confidentiality and conflict-of-interest agreement, required before either request is considered.

When you apply for ethics review

  • Your study protocol, consent materials, and any supporting documents you upload;
  • Institutional/IRB details, supervisor name and contact (for student applicants);
  • Payment confirmation for any applicable review fee — processed by Paystack; we do not receive or store your card details.

Automatically, to keep your account secure

  • Session information: your browser/device string, approximate IP address, and sign-in time, shown back to you in your dashboard's Active Sessions panel;
  • Basic technical logs needed to operate and secure the Platform.

When you contact us

  • Whatever you include in a message through our Contact page, so we can reply to it.
03

How we use it

To run the review, keep the record, and email you when something changes. Never to build an ad profile.

  • Operating the review process — routing applications to the right reviewers and Committee agenda, tracking status, recording decisions.
  • Account & role administration — verifying eligibility for Reviewer/Committee access, and letting an admin approve, reject, or (if needed) suspend an account.
  • Communications — sending status updates, decision letters, role-request confirmations, and, where you've opted in, new-sign-in notifications, by email via our transactional email provider.
  • The verification service — confirming approval status for a given reference number when someone uses our public Verify Approval tool.
  • Payments — reconciling review fees against your application, through Paystack.
  • Security & abuse prevention — detecting suspicious sign-ins and enforcing our Acceptable Use terms.
  • Legal & governance obligations — keeping the records our Records Policy and applicable research-governance rules require.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

04

Who we share it with

People who need it to do the review, and the handful of processors that keep MSREC running. Nobody else.

  • Assigned Reviewers & Committee Members see the application content needed to assess your study — nothing from other applicants, and nothing outside their assignment.
  • The Chair and Secretariat can access applications and account records to administer the review process.
  • Your named supervisor may be contacted directly where your application identifies one and their input is part of the review.

We also rely on a small number of processors to run the Platform, each bound to use your data only to provide their service to us:

ProcessorWhat they handle
SupabaseHosts our database and the private file storage for uploaded documents (CVs, profile photos, supporting files).
PaystackProcesses review-fee payments. Card details go directly to Paystack, not to MSREC.
ResendDelivers transactional email — decision letters, confirmations, notifications.

Outside of these, we disclose personal data only where required by law, to protect the rights or safety of MSREC or others, or with your explicit consent.

05

Confidentiality of applications & reviews

Review content is need-to-know, always. Reviewers and Committee members are bound to keep it that way.

Every Reviewer and Committee Member agrees to our Confidentiality Policy before their access is approved: protocol content, applicant data, and Committee deliberations may be used only to complete the review they've been assigned, and may not be discussed, published, or reused outside that purpose. Conflicts of interest are declared and recused before a review begins, under our Conflict of Interest Policy. Both policies are published in full on our Governance page.

06

Cookies & similar technologies

One cookie, to keep you signed in. No ad trackers, no cross-site profiling.

MSREC sets a single essential session cookie so the Platform can recognize you as signed in as you move between pages; without it, dashboards simply wouldn't work. We don't use third-party advertising or analytics cookies. Pages do load webfonts from Google Fonts, which involves your browser requesting font files directly from Google's servers — standard for most modern websites, and unrelated to your MSREC account.

07

Data retention

Decided applications become part of the permanent research record and are kept accordingly, even after you close your account.

How long we keep information depends on what it is, set out in full in our Records Policy on the Governance page. As a general rule: application files, review reports, and Committee decisions are retained for the period our research-governance obligations require, since a decided ethics review needs to remain verifiable years later — including through our public Verify Approval tool. Account profile information is kept for as long as your account is active, and for a limited period afterward to satisfy legal and audit requirements, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

08

International data transfers

Our processors operate internationally. We only use ones contractually bound to protect your data wherever it sits.

MSREC is based in Ghana, but the infrastructure providers described in Section 4 — Supabase, Paystack, and Resend — may process or store data in other countries. Where that happens, we rely on their contractual and technical safeguards to protect your data to a standard consistent with this notice, regardless of where it's processed.

09

Data security

Passwords are hashed, connections are encrypted, access is role-gated. No system is unbreachable, but this is how we reduce the risk.

Passwords are never stored in plain text — only as a salted, one-way hash that can't be reversed even by us. Traffic to the Platform is encrypted in transit, and application content is only reachable by accounts with a role-based reason to see it. Your dashboard's Profile & Security page also lets you review active sessions and set sign-in notification preferences; two-factor authentication toggles are available there as a stated preference, though this is not yet enforced as a mandatory sign-in step. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we design deliberately to limit what's exposed and to whom.

If we ever become aware of a data breach affecting your personal information, we'll notify affected account holders and relevant authorities as required by law.

10

Your rights & choices

Access, correct, download, or ask us to delete what we hold on you, subject to our record-keeping obligations.

  • Access & correction. Most of your profile is editable directly from your dashboard's Profile & Security page; for anything else, ask the Secretariat.
  • Portability. You can request an export of the personal data we hold on your account.
  • Deletion. You can ask us to close your account and delete your personal data, subject to the retention obligations in Section 7 — a decided application's record can't be erased on request, since it's part of the research-governance record, not just your personal profile.
  • Communication preferences. The new-sign-in notification toggle on your Profile & Security page is yours to switch off; transactional emails tied to an active application (decisions, requests for revision) can't be disabled, since they're how we tell you what's happening with your review.
  • Objection. Where our use of your data relies on legitimate interest rather than a contractual necessity, you may object to that specific use.

To exercise any of these, email secretariat@msrec.org or use our Contact page. We'll respond within a reasonable time, and may need to verify your identity first.

11

Children's privacy

MSREC is intended for researchers, institutions, and professionals aged 18 and over, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under that age. If a study protocol itself involves research participants who are minors, that data is handled under the study's own consent and safeguarding protocols, reviewed by the Committee as part of the application — not as account data covered by this notice.

12

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as our practices evolve. The "Last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version, and we'll email active account holders ahead of any change that materially affects how your data is handled.

13

Contact & complaints

For any question about this notice or how your data is handled, contact the MSREC Secretariat at secretariat@msrec.org or through our Contact page. If you believe we haven't resolved your concern, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
