04 — Cost

Fees

Fees fund administrative screening and reviewer coordination — never the decision itself.

Review Type Fee Note
Ethics Determination / Exemption GHS 200 Commonly waived for unfunded student-led studies
Expedited Review GHS 500 —
Full Committee Review GHS 950 Covers one meeting cycle
Amendment / Modification Review GHS 150 Per amendment request
Continuing Review / Progress Report GHS 100 Annual, per approved study

Payment of a review fee does not guarantee ethical approval, and cannot expedite or influence a Committee decision.
