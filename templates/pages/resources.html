Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you reach out to the Secretariat.

You don't have to decide alone — select "Not Sure, MSREC to Determine" on the Application Form and the Secretariat will classify your study as Ethics Determination/Exemption, Expedited, or Full Committee Review during screening.

Ethics Determination and Expedited reviews are typically completed faster than Full Committee Review, which follows the Committee's scheduled meeting calendar. Turnaround also depends on how complete your initial submission is.

Use the Amendment / Modification Request Form under Application Forms above. Substantive changes must be reviewed and approved before they are implemented.

Use Verify Approval to look up any MSREC approval by its approval number, verification code, or QR code, and see its current, live status.

It isn't mandatory for every applicant category, but the Committee strongly recommends completing at least Foundations of Research Ethics beforehand — see Training below.

Use the Adult template for participants who can consent for themselves, the Parental/Guardian template alongside an Assent form when enrolling minors, and always pair a consent form with a Participant Information Sheet.

The Secretariat logs and triages the report immediately. Serious or unexpected events may trigger a request for corrective action, additional monitoring, or in rare cases, suspension of the approval pending investigation.
