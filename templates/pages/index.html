Frequently Asked Questions

These answers are general guidance only. If your project's status is uncertain, request a formal ethics determination rather than relying on this page as a decision.

1. Do I need ethics review for an online survey?

Possibly. Research type alone does not determine whether formal approval is required; submit a request so MSREC, not the applicant, can issue a formal determination.

2. Is review required for previously collected or published data?

Often a shorter determination pathway applies, but applicants should not self-declare exemption. MSREC confirms whether individual-level, restricted or unpublished identifiable data trigger full review.

3. Does my AI or machine-learning project need review?

AI-related review is risk-based. Projects using human data, producing automated outputs that affect individuals, or lacking human oversight will generally require review of data provenance, bias, safety and oversight.

4. Can payment of a review fee speed up or guarantee approval?

No. Review fees are non-refundable administrative charges and are never a condition of approval. Protocol-specific decisions remain independent of payment.

5. How long does a decision take?

Timelines depend on the assigned pathway (ethics determination, expedited review or full Committee review) and on the completeness of the submission and reviewer availability.
