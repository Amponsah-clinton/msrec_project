How It Works

From account to decision in four steps.

Create your account

Sign up for a free applicant account — it only takes a minute.

Complete the form

Fill in the Universal Application Form from your dashboard, saving drafts as you go.

Submit for review

MSREC screens your submission and assigns the appropriate review pathway.

Track your decision

Follow your study's status, respond to queries and download letters — all in one place.

What You'll Need

Have this ready before you start

Gathering these in advance makes the form quicker to complete.

Study details

Title, purpose, design and methodology of your research.

PI & research team

Principal investigator, co-investigators and supervisor information.

Risk & consent plan

How participants are recruited, consented and protected.

Supporting documents

Protocol, consent forms and any recruitment materials, ready to upload.

Review Pathways

MSREC will confirm which pathway fits your study

You'll be asked to indicate a preference on the form — the Secretariat makes the final determination during screening.

Ethics Determination / Exemption

For studies likely to qualify for a lighter-touch assessment.

Expedited Review

Minimal-risk studies reviewed by the Chair and designated reviewers.

Full Committee Review

More than minimal risk, reviewed at the next monthly meeting.
