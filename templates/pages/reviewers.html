The Reviewer's Role

Independent assessment, matched to the study — separate from the Secretariat's administrative screening and the Committee's governance.

Once the Secretariat confirms an application is complete, it's assigned to one or more reviewers whose declared expertise matches the study's discipline and review pathway. A reviewer works through a structured Ethical Review Assessment Form for every application, declares any conflict of interest before opening the file, and submits a recommendation — not a unilateral approval on their own authority alone for studies referred to full Committee review.

What's Assessed

The same ten-point ethical review checklist behind every reviewer's assessment, whatever the discipline.

Scientific Rationale

Whether the research question and design are sound enough to justify the study at all.

Risks & Benefits

Whether risks to participants are minimized and justified by the study's potential benefit.

Participant Selection

Whether recruitment and selection are fair, with no group unduly burdened or excluded.

Informed Consent

Whether the consent process and materials genuinely inform, in language participants can follow.

Privacy & Data Handling

How data is collected, stored, accessed and eventually disposed of, and who can see it.

Vulnerable Participants

Extra safeguards where participants have reduced capacity to protect their own interests.

Join the Panel

MSREC recruits reviewers across health, social science, engineering, AI/ICT and evidence-synthesis disciplines.

Relevant Expertise

Postgraduate qualification or equivalent professional standing in a discipline MSREC reviews.

Research Ethics Familiarity

Working knowledge of human-participant research ethics, or willingness to complete MSREC's orientation.

Time to Commit

Capacity to turn around an assigned assessment within roughly 10 working days.
