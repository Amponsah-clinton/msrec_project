Board Structure

Institutional governance sits above the Committee, but stops short of touching its decisions.

MetaScholar Limited's Board provides institutional governance for MSREC — legal standing, financial stewardship, appointments and resourcing. It has no authority to direct, review or reverse a protocol-specific ethics decision; that authority sits entirely with the Committee.

Chairperson
Elected by the Board from among its independent members
Composition
3 members, a majority of whom are independent of MetaScholar management
Term length
3 years, renewable once
Appointment
Board appoints and reappoints Committee members and the Committee Chairperson
Board Institutional governance
Governs & resources — does not direct decisions
Committee Independent ethics decisions
Administrative support, both ways
Secretariat Screening, records & scheduling

Board Responsibilities

What institutional governance covers — and, just as importantly, what it deliberately stays out of.

Institutional Governance

Holds legal, financial and reputational responsibility for MSREC's operation within MetaScholar Limited.

Appointments

Appoints and reappoints the Committee Chairperson and members, and confirms Secretariat leadership.

Charter & Policy Approval

Approves the MSREC Charter, standard operating procedures and major policy changes.

Resourcing

Ensures the Committee and Secretariat are adequately staffed, trained and funded to review without delay.

Periodic Reporting

Receives aggregate reporting on review activity, timelines and adverse events — never individual protocol files.

Does Not Direct Decisions

Cannot approve, deny, expedite or reverse a specific protocol decision. That authority belongs to the Committee alone.

Committee Composition

A multidisciplinary panel, built so no single perspective can dominate a decision.

Every application is reviewed by members spanning at least these seven areas of expertise. At least one member is independent of any applicant's institution, and at least one is a lay or community representative with no research affiliation.

Health & Biomedical Science Social & Behavioral Science Research Methodology & Biostatistics Law, Data Protection & Privacy Ethics & Bioethics Lay / Community Representative Independent External Member
5 Quorum — minimum members present to decide
3 yrs Standard Committee term, renewable once
1+ Lay / independent member required for quorum

Secretariat

The administrative engine that keeps applications moving — without ever deciding one.

Administrative Screening

Checks every submission for completeness before it reaches a reviewer, and requests missing documents directly.

Scheduling

Builds the meeting agenda, assigns reviewers by expertise, and coordinates Committee availability.

Records & Minutes

Maintains the official record of every application, decision, amendment and meeting minute.

Correspondence

Issues outcome letters, amendment acknowledgements and reviewer queries on the Committee's behalf.

Applicant Support

First point of contact for questions on forms, templates, status and the Verify Approval tool.

Cannot Grant Approval

Administrative screening or fee payment is never a substitute for Committee review — the Secretariat cannot approve a study.

Meeting Arrangements

How and when the Committee actually sits down to decide.

Meeting Type Frequency Notice / Cut-off Quorum
Regular Committee Meeting Second Thursday of every month Complete applications due 10 working days prior 5 members, incl. 1 lay representative
Expedited Review Rolling, asynchronous Decision within 10 working days of assignment Chair or Vice-Chair + 2 designated reviewers
Special / Emergency Meeting As needed Convened within 5 working days of a trigger event 5 members, incl. Chair or Vice-Chair
Annual Governance Review Once a year Board and Committee leadership, scheduled in advance Board quorum applies

Minutes & Records

Draft minutes circulate within 5 working days and are ratified at the following meeting, then retained under MSREC's document retention policy.

Decision Communication

Outcome letters are issued within 5 working days of a decision, and the record becomes visible through Verify Approval.

Format

Meetings run hybrid by default — in person at the Secretariat office, with a virtual option for members joining remotely.
