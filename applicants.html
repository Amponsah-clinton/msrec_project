Who Needs Review

Research that requires MSREC's approval

Research type alone doesn't determine whether formal approval is required — MSREC makes that determination, not the applicant. These are the domains most commonly reviewed.

Health & Biomedical

Clinical trials, public health studies and biomedical or health-data research.

Social & Behavioral

Surveys, interviews and classroom, institutional or behavioral studies.

Business & Engineering

Organizational, workplace and applied engineering studies.

AI, ICT & Systems

Machine learning, digital platforms, software and HCI evaluation.

Secondary Data

Registries, existing datasets, systematic/scoping reviews and bibliometrics.

Online & Digital

Internet-mediated studies, online surveys and digital fieldwork.

Eligibility

Who can apply directly

Most applicants can proceed straight to submission. A few situations call for one extra step first.

Apply directly if…

  • You're a registered student, faculty or staff member at a MetaScholar-affiliated institution.
  • You're an independent researcher with a clearly defined protocol.
  • Your research is sponsored by a recognized institution or organization.
  • Your study has a named Principal Investigator.

One extra step if…

  • You're an undergraduate student — requires supervisor co-signature.
  • Your institution isn't a MetaScholar partner — requires a collaboration agreement.
  • Your study involves a vulnerable population — requires additional safeguarding review.
  • Your study is externally sponsored — requires a data-sharing agreement on file.
Requirements

What reviewers look for

Every protocol is assessed against three categories of requirement, regardless of discipline.

Ethical

  • Informed consent process clearly defined
  • Risk-benefit balance assessed
  • Confidentiality & data protection plan
  • Safeguards for vulnerable populations, where relevant

Methodological

  • Clear research design and objectives
  • Justified sample size and selection method
  • Validated instruments and tools
  • Defined data analysis plan

Administrative

  • Completed application form
  • Institutional support letter, where applicable
  • Supervisor sign-off for student applicants
  • Proof of ethics training, where required

Review Types

Four tracks, matched to risk

MSREC assigns a review track during screening — you don't need to select one yourself.

For minimal-risk studies using existing, de-identified data, or standard educational-practice evaluations. Screened and determined by the Secretariat — no full Committee vote required.

Typical turnaround: 5–7 business days

For minimal-risk studies that involve direct participant contact but present low complexity. Reviewed by one or two designated reviewers rather than the full Committee.

Typical turnaround: 10–12 business days

For studies involving more than minimal risk, vulnerable populations, or sensitive data. Reviewed and voted on by the full multidisciplinary Committee at a scheduled meeting.

Typical turnaround: 15–21 business days

For changes to an already-approved protocol — procedures, personnel or consent materials. Reviewed at a level proportional to the scale of the change.

Typical turnaround: 5–10 business days
